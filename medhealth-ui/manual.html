Navigation Manual

Step-by-step guide for patients, providers, claims, payments, reports, and audit.

Open Login Open App

1. Start Here

Frontendhttp://localhost:8000
Backendhttp://localhost:8001
Swaggerhttp://localhost:8001/docs
  1. Open http://localhost:8000.
  2. Select your role from the login screen.
  3. Enter your username and password, then click Continue.
  4. After login, use the left-side navigation to move between Dashboard, Patients, Providers, Claims, Payments, Reports, Audit, Users, and Settings.
  5. Use the User Manual button in the top bar whenever you need to return to this guide.
Recommended demo accounts: admin / admin123, billing / billing123, provider / provider123, finance / finance123, auditor / auditor123.

2. Patient Onboarding

Use this flow to register a patient record before creating or processing claims.

  1. From the left navigation, click Patients.
  2. Click New in the top-right corner.
  3. Capture the patient details: Name, Medical Record Number, Date of Birth, Email, Phone.
  4. Click Create.
  5. Confirm the patient appears in the table and note the MRN for future reference.
  6. Use Edit if you need to update contact details or status later.
Best practice: onboard the patient first so the claims team has a valid patient ID before opening a claim.

3. Provider Onboarding

Use Providers to register doctors, practices, or facilities that will appear on claims.

  1. Click Providers in the left navigation.
  2. Click New.
  3. Capture: Name, NPI, Specialty, Email, Phone.
  4. Click Create.
  5. Verify the provider is visible in the list and note the provider ID or NPI if you will use it in a claim.
  6. Use Edit to correct specialty or contact information when needed.
Providers should be created before claims so claim capture can link a valid provider ID.

4. Claim Processing

Claims move through readiness, closure, validation, payload generation, submission, remittance, and evidence review.

ClosureClose fileImmutable snapshot is created

4.1 Create a New Claim

  1. Go to Claims.
  2. Click New.
  3. Capture: Claim Number, Patient ID, Provider ID, Amount.
  4. Click Create.
  5. Click Open on the claim row to enter the claim cockpit.

4.2 Run Readiness

  1. In Claim detail, click Run readiness.
  2. Read the summary panel on the right: PASS, WARN, BLOCKED.
  3. If the claim passes or warns, proceed to closure.
  4. If the claim is blocked, stop and review the reason code before continuing.
Example from your screenshot: Closure result for CLM-1002: blocked. ICD_MISSING. This means the claim failed readiness because the primary ICD-10 diagnosis is missing, so the system will not allow closure.

4.3 Close the Claim

  1. Click Close file.
  2. If closure succeeds, the claim is frozen into a billing snapshot.
  3. If closure is blocked or requires override, read the popup message and resolve the issue before retrying.

4.4 Post-Closure Validate

  1. Click Post-closure validate.
  2. Wait for the post-closure status to update to VALID, WARNING, or INVALID.
  3. If valid or warning, continue to payload generation.

4.5 Build Payload

  1. Click Build payload.
  2. Review the Canonical payload preview on the left.
  3. Review the Pseudo-EDI preview on the right.
  4. Use this step to confirm member number, scheme, diagnosis, and billed amount before submission.

4.6 Submit the Claim

  1. Click Submit (Direct) when the scheme should be reached directly.
  2. Click Submit (Switch) when the claim should be routed through a switch.
  3. Review the returned status: ACK, REJ, PEND.
  4. Use the outcome to decide whether to correct and resubmit, upload supporting evidence, or wait for remittance.

4.7 Remittance and Evidence

  1. Click View remittance to inspect payment status and references.
  2. Click Evidence packet to review the full lifecycle chain: snapshot, decision bundles, payloads, submissions, responses, remittance, reconciliation, audit events.

5. Payments

Use Payments to log or review EFTs and other claim-linked payments.

  1. Go to Payments.
  2. Click New.
  3. Capture: Claim ID, Amount, Payment Date, Method.
  4. Click Create.
  5. Use Edit to update payment status when the payment clears or needs correction.
  6. Cross-check payment activity with claim remittance results.

6. Reports

Reports show operational totals, rule-hit trends, and audit or payment analysis.

  1. Go to Reports.
  2. Click New.
  3. Select a report type, for example: summary, payment, audit.
  4. Enter the reporting period.
  5. Click Generate.
  6. Use Download on the row to inspect the generated report details.

7. Audit and Evidence

Use Audit to trace who did what, when it happened, and what policy or decision data was involved.

  1. Go to Audit.
  2. Review the table columns: Action, Resource, User, Timestamp, Details.
  3. Look for events such as: READINESS_RUN, SNAPSHOT_CREATED, CLAIM_SUBMITTED, RESPONSE_RECEIVED, RECONCILED.
  4. For claim-specific investigations, open the claim and use Evidence packet from the claim cockpit.

9. Troubleshooting and Common Messages

Reason codeMeaningWhat to do next
ICD_MISSINGPrimary diagnosis missingCapture or correct the ICD-10 before closure.
DISCHARGE_MISSINGInpatient claim has no discharge dateUpdate the inpatient encounter details before closure.
PREAUTH_MISSINGRequired authorisation not presentCapture a valid authorisation or check policy rules.
MEMBER_INVALIDMember number format rejected on submissionCorrect the member number and submit a new claim version.
ATTACHMENT_REQUIREDSupporting document is missingUpload motivation or report and resubmit if required.
MODIFIER_SEQUENCE_INVALIDModifier sequence failed validationCorrect the line structure before building the payload again.
If a claim is blocked, do not continue to payload or submission. Resolve the blocking reason first, then rerun readiness or validation.
